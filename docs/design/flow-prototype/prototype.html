Motion spec

One authored moment (the opening) and one continuity move (card expand). Everything else is fast state feedback. All timings scale with the slow-motion switch above. Easing names: out-expo = cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1). Only transform, opacity and clip-path animate, so nothing reflows mid-move.

Review log (design-craft critique and audit)

Version 1 was scored, audited in a headless browser (computed contrast, accessible names, duplicate ids, heading order, target size, language mixing, keyboard-only walk with real Tab, Enter and Esc) and then fixed. Heuristic score went from 28/40 to 33/40.

PriFoundFix in this version
P1Collapsed cards showed scores with no source link, which breaks the "no number without a source" rule.Every card has "Źródła: n" as a link that opens the card at its sources.
P1Results and card views had no h1 (the search title was display: none).The h1 stays in the DOM as a visually hidden "Szukaj innowacji" in the results view.
P1After switching to English, the live region still held a Polish announcement.The live region is cleared on every language switch.
P1Phone: hero text sat on the busy constellation render; phone: the card's bottom bar took a third of the screen (three stacked buttons).Phone hero moves the render up, dims it and adds a stronger scrim. The bar is one row: icon back, icon owner link, primary compare.
P2Keyboard users met about 15 filter controls before the first result.Skip link "Przejdź do wyników (n)" at the top of the filter column, visible on focus. "/" focuses search.
P2Collapsed cards were about 330 px tall, 2 per screen.Level, TRL and scores share one meta row with one estimate badge; source and date in one footer line.
P2Empty state blamed the query when a filter caused it.Copy says "z wybranymi filtrami" when filters are on.
P2Hero headline wrapped every 1 to 2 words (max-width in ch of a 16 px parent).Max width set in px.
P2Inline refs [1] and jump links were 18 to 23 px tall.Min height 26 to 32 px.
P3Dead corner left of the search bar in results; double search icon; truncated placeholder on phone; duplicate "Filtry" heading in the phone sheet; "Załóż konto" on its own line.All fixed.
