## Showcase 1: A bone substitute the surgeon shapes at the operating table
*A bioresorbable polylactide scaffold for cancellous bone, protected by a family of granted Polish patents held by Politechnika Warszawska*

Critical bone defects, left by trauma, tumour removal or osteoporosis, do not heal on their own. Today they are filled with bone taken from the patient, which means a second wound, or with rigid substitutes that must fit a shape decided before surgery. A team at Politechnika Warszawska, working with the company BoneReg, has built a porous polylactide implant that the surgeon cuts and presses into the defect during the operation. It soaks up the patient's own platelet-rich plasma, and it is gradually resorbed and replaced by new bone. The method is protected by a family of six granted Polish patents. In December 2025 it won the main prize of Poland's national innovation contest, Polski Produkt Przyszłości.

### How it works

1. **Scaffold.** Polylactide (PLA), a biodegradable polymer, is dissolved in dioxane, frozen and set in a gelling bath (phase inversion with freeze extraction). The result is a sponge with the architecture of cancellous bone: pores of 100–400 µm and 94% open porosity.
2. **Making a water-repelling polymer take up liquids.** PLA naturally repels water. The patents add hydrophilic components: methacrylic-acid copolymers, a calcium-phosphate coating, or natural polysaccharides (chitosan, sodium alginate, sodium hyaluronate) pressed in under vacuum. The scaffold can then absorb about 8.5 times its dry weight.
3. **At the operating table.** The surgeon shapes the flexible implant to the defect and soaks it in platelet-rich plasma (PRP) prepared from the patient's own blood, which supplies growth factors. Over time the implant is resorbed and replaced by new bone, so it never has to be removed.

### How to grow it (our proposals)

- **Plan the medical-device route now.** A resorbable implant is a medical device under the EU MDR, and devices of this kind usually fall in the highest risk class. That means a notified body and a clinical investigation. Planning the technical file, biocompatibility tests and sterilisation validation in parallel with the sheep study saves a year or more. The team already publishes with the radiosterilisation facility of the Institute of Nuclear Chemistry and Technology (IChTJ), a natural partner for sterilisation. *Our proposal; the risk class should be confirmed by a regulatory specialist.*
- **Start with veterinary use.** Animal implants face lighter rules than human ones. A veterinary product for horses or dogs could earn revenue and real-world data while human trials run. The researchers already mention it. *Our proposal.*
- **An antibiotic-releasing version.** Infected defects (osteomyelitis) are a large unmet need. The same group has published on neomycin-releasing polylactide spheres. Combining the two would give a local antibiotic carrier that resorbs. *Our proposal, building on the group's 2021 paper.*
- **A PRP kit.** Patent PL 239154 already covers soaking in platelet-rich plasma. Partnering with makers of PRP centrifuges would give hospitals one validated kit: scaffold plus PRP protocol. *Our proposal.*
- **Patient-specific shapes for large defects.** For tumour resections, scaffolds could be cast in moulds printed from the patient's CT scan, adding custom shapes to the at-table trimming. *Our proposal.*
- **Protect improvements abroad.** All six patents are Polish only. Their priority years have passed, so foreign protection is possible only for new improvements. Each new development should be filed under the PCT before it is published. *Our note on patent procedure.*
- **Fund the clinical step.** Clinical investigations are expensive. Public calls for clinical research, such as those of the Polish Medical Research Agency (ABM), and EU instruments such as the EIC Accelerator are the usual next funding steps after NCBR LIDER. *Our proposal.*
- **Show the saving.** If the PLN 200 price holds, against PLN 600–800 per defect today, a health-economics (HTA) dossier built from the trial data would help hospitals and the National Health Fund (NFZ) adopt it. *Our proposal; the price is the team's 2021 estimate.*

### Limits and open questions

- **Not yet in patients.** The implant is at the preclinical stage (sheep), and no clinical trial is registered in our sources. The Oslo test is not passed until it is available. The card must say „testing / preclinical”.
- **Several claims are the developer's.** The anti-inflammatory and antibacterial action, the PLN 200 price and the industry interest come from the developer and the university, not from independent tests. They are labelled as such.
- **Grant dates.** The grant dates in the table are the UPRP decision dates supplied by the reviewer from the register. The B1 dates are the patent publication dates on Google Patents. Both are given.
- **Who owns what.** The patents belong to Politechnika Warszawska. BoneReg's licence terms are not public, so the card should name PW as owner and BoneReg as development partner.
- **Two products, one project.** The 2021 university article also describes a second, injectable filler for osteoporotic bone. This showcase covers only the flexible scaffold.

## Showcase 2: e-Doręczenia: the registered letter, made digital
*Poland's national electronic registered delivery service, legally equal to a paper letter with proof of receipt*

Until recently, an official letter in Poland meant a paper registered letter with proof of receipt („polecony za potwierdzeniem odbioru”), a slip in the letterbox and a queue at the post office. e-Doręczenia replaces it with an electronic registered delivery that has the same legal force. Since 1 January 2026 it is the default channel between public administration and private parties, and it has passed 100 million deliveries. It is the clearest example in our database of an administrative innovation that is already in everyday use at national scale.

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Area** | Administrative |
| **Owner** | Ministerstwo Cyfryzacji (service owner); Poczta Polska S.A. as designated operator until 2035 |
| **Region** | Nationwide. Address counts per voivodeship are published |
| **Polish IP** | Not applicable (a public service, not a patented technology) |
| **Implementation level** | In practice (confirmed) |
| **TRL** | Not applicable (administrative) |
| **Oslo innovation test** | Passed (estimate): new, significantly different, in use |
| **Legal basis** | Act of 18 November 2020 on electronic delivery (Dz.U. 2020 poz. 2320) and the EU eIDAS regulation |

### How it works

1. Every citizen, company and institution can get an electronic delivery address and a mailbox, on edoreczenia.gov.pl or, since 1 July 2026, in the mObywatel app.
2. A letter sent through the public registered delivery service (PURDE) has the legal force of a registered letter with proof of receipt. Sending and receiving are confirmed with a qualified electronic time stamp, so both sides get legally valid proof of who sent what, and when.
3. If the addressee has no electronic address yet, an office uses the public hybrid service (PUH): the electronic document goes to the post, where it is printed automatically and delivered on paper. Offices can therefore switch to digital before their recipients do.

### How to grow it (our proposals)

- **Move the hybrid volume to digital.** In May 2026, 46 of 78 million deliveries were still printed. At the current price list an office pays PLN 8.35 for a hybrid letter and PLN 5.30 for an electronic one, so every letter moved to digital saves PLN 3.05. At the May volume that is roughly PLN 140 million, an indicative figure we computed from the published prices (past prices may have differed). Feature idea: when a hybrid letter is sent, the paper copy invites the addressee to set up an address in mObywatel in two minutes. *Our proposal.*
- **Explain letters in plain language.** Official letters are hard to read. The mObywatel app already has a virtual assistant built on the Polish model PLLuM. Linking it to the e-Doręczenia inbox, so that it explains what a letter means and what to do, would serve the people who need it most. *Our proposal.*
- **Automatic deadline calendar.** Every delivery carries a qualified time stamp, and the draft amendment of July 2026 introduces new rules for counting deadlines. The app could compute and remind the user of the reply deadline for each letter. *Our proposal.*
- **Use the cross-border access point.** The legal framework already provides an access point for registered electronic delivery across borders (eIDAS). Promoting it to Poles abroad and to EU companies dealing with Polish offices would extend reach at no new legal cost. *Our proposal, based on the legal acts listed by the service.*
- **Target the regions that lag.** The ministry publishes addresses per voivodeship (5.77 million on 01.11.2026). Dividing by the GUS population (our R9 regional data) shows which regions lag, so information campaigns can be aimed there. *Our proposal.*
- **Open monthly statistics.** Publishing deliveries, the hybrid share and addresses monthly on dane.gov.pl would let anyone track the switch to digital, including Pomysłownik! itself. *Our proposal.*
- **Bring the courts in earlier.** Courts and prosecutors join only from 1.10.2029. Pilots with selected courts before then would test the largest remaining stream of registered letters. *Our proposal.*

### Limits and open questions

- **Most deliveries still end on paper.** Of the 78 million deliveries counted in May 2026, 46 million were hybrid (printed by the post) and 32 million fully electronic. The service is digital at the office's end long before it is digital at the citizen's end. We do not count this against it: the hybrid service is how the switch can happen at all.
- **The numbers are the ministry's own.** They come from press releases and the ministry's published dataset, not from an independent audit. The regional total of 5,769,965 is our sum of the ministry's per-voivodeship figures.
- **Units differ between releases.** Releases speak of mailboxes (May), addresses (August) and per-type addresses (November dataset). The card should quote each figure with its date and unit and not mix them.
- **Not finished.** Courts and prosecutors join only from 2029, and the act is being amended (draft of 7 July 2026).
- **The act itself is linked, not quoted.** ISAP and Dziennik Ustaw block automated access in robots.txt, so the act's text was not fetched. Its title, date and role are quoted from the ministry's pages.

### In the demo

1. Choose the „Public bodies” audience and filter Area = administrative. Open e-Doręczenia.
2. Show **In practice, confirmed** next to the quote that names the scale: 100 million deliveries.
3. Open the evidence: the 78 → 100 million growth in under three months and the hybrid/digital split. Each number is quoted with its date.
4. Show the regional numbers (per voivodeship, 01.11.2026). They connect to the regional context view (R9).
